---
name: Collective shopping-list approval
description: Approval and points semantics for shared shopping-list tasks.
---

A shopping list is completed and approved as one collective task, not as separate item or contributor completions. The parent sees one approval request; approving or rejecting it applies to every assigned recipient.

Every assigned member receives the task's full point value after collective approval. Do not divide points based on who checked individual products. For a family-wide list without explicit assignments, use the members who actually contributed.

**Why:** Individual products are collaborative steps toward one list. Separate approval cards and split point amounts make one task appear as several unrelated completions and require repetitive parent decisions.

**How to apply:** Keep per-member accounting records internally when needed for balances, history, and achievements, but group them into one logical approval action and finalize the list only after that collective decision.

On TV, submitted shopping lists use the same amber/brown card appearance as other submitted tasks, for both parent and child profiles. Keeping the list accessible does not make it an open or approved task.

**Why:** The user expects the complete list to visibly enter the submitted workflow while it waits for collective parent approval.

**How to apply:** Keep list-interaction eligibility separate from workflow appearance. Submission coloring remains visible even when generic task submission is disabled; reopening after unchecking restores the open appearance.

Erledigte einmalige Einkaufslisten gehören nicht als lesbare Historie in den TV-Aufgabenbereich, auch nicht unter „Wichtig“. Offene und auf Genehmigung wartende Listen bleiben sichtbar.

**Why:** Der Nutzer hat ausdrücklich beanstandet, dass die abgeschlossene einmalige Einkaufsliste am Fernseher weiterhin angezeigt wurde.

**How to apply:** Die kollektive Fertigstellung für die Sichtbarkeit berücksichtigen; einzelne unvollständige Mitgliederzeilen dürfen eine abgeschlossene einmalige Liste nicht wieder anzeigen.
