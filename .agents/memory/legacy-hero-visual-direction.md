name: Legacy hero visual direction
description: Approved visual direction for modernizing the star-unlocked Legacy heroes.
---

Modern Legacy heroes should use polished, contemporary 2.5D children’s game art with strong silhouettes, expressive faces, dynamic poses, and diverse representation. Keep turquoise/aqua, warm gold-orange, and deep navy as the dominant palette so the collection remains visually tied to the Little Champs logo. Give every hero a distinct positive theme or power, and design matching environments with the identifying landmark concentrated in the center for narrow phone crops.

**Why:** The user explicitly approved the first modern shield-hero concept and then requested the complete 12-character Legacy collection in this direction on September 12, 2026.

**How to apply:** Use this direction for future Legacy additions or revisions. Keep avatar art on white, environments character-free, and central composition intact. Show concepts first unless implementation is explicitly requested.
