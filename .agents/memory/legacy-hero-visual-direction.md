description: Approved visual direction for modernizing the star-unlocked Legacy heroes.

Legacy heroes should use warm polished 3D storybook illustration with rounded child proportions, expressive joyful faces, softly rendered deep-navy outlines, readable costumes, and moderate detail. Keep turquoise/aqua, warm gold, coral, lavender, mint, and gentle pastel tones. Avoid futuristic armor, neon, realism, and overly flat mascot art. Matching environments use the same classic magical-adventure style with the identifying landmark centered for narrow phone crops. Use the visual density of the Shadow Ninja environment as the detail benchmark: layered architecture, tactile materials, atmospheric lighting, environmental props, and clear foreground, middle ground, and background depth, while keeping every environment specific to its hero.

**Why:** After comparing futuristic, highly simplified, and intermediate concepts, the user explicitly selected the warm storybook style of their supplied Little Champs ensemble artwork on September 12, 2026.

**How to apply:** Use the supplied ensemble-style direction for future Legacy additions or revisions. Keep avatar art on white, environments character-free, and central composition intact. Preserve agreed cast diversity when regenerating the collection. Do not copy the ninja theme into other worlds; transfer only its richness, spatial depth, materials, and density of meaningful details.

Kometensprint is intentionally represented by a refreshed version of the blonde, unmasked female superhero from the Little Champs logo, with turquoise suit, gold cape, and lightning emblem.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose the female logo hero to replace the forest-elf version of this Legacy character.

**How to apply:** Preserve this identity and logo-derived costume whenever Kometensprint artwork is revised.
