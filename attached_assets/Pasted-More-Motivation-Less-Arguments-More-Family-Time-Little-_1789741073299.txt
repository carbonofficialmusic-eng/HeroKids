More Motivation. Less Arguments. More Family Time.

Little Champs is the fun family app for chores, routines and rewards. Kids complete household tasks, earn points, unlock cool avatars and backgrounds, and build responsibility through play.

Whether it's cleaning their room, setting the table, doing homework or brushing their teeth, Little Champs turns daily chores into exciting missions and helps families build routines and healthy habits without constant reminders.

WHY LITTLE CHAMPS?

• Kids love collecting and unlocking

With every completed task, kids earn points, unlock new characters, backgrounds and rewards, and stay motivated over time.

• Less stress for parents

Create chores, routines and family goals in seconds while keeping track of progress, points and rewards. From daily habits to fully customizable recurring tasks, Little Champs offers maximum flexibility for every family.

• Work together towards family goals

Collect points together and unlock special experiences such as a theme park trip, movie night, zoo visit or other family rewards. Turn teamwork into a fun family adventure.

• Better family organization

A powerful chore planner, family chat, shopping list, message board and reward system all in one app. Assign tasks to individual family members, multiple selected family members or the entire family and keep everyday life organized with ease.
